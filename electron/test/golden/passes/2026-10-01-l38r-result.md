# Result: L38R, the Live router probe (a spike; reported, never a ship decision)

Written 2026-10-01, 15:21 local. The rule: `PREREGISTER-l38r.md`, written 15:04, before the first call. Both reps ran
15:05–15:12 and 15:13–15:20, one Live session per item, 44 sessions, 0 abnormal closes, 0 retries.

## The question

Can bare `gemini-3.8-live`, told to answer a short one-part factual question in one line and to say "hard" for
anything else, tell the two apart, put the right answer in that line, and do it well before the pipeline's first cue?

## The reading, by the five bars (`runs/r1.read.txt`, `runs/r2.read.txt`)

| bar | rep 1 | rep 2 | pooled | met? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. simple routed, ≥ 10 of 12 per rep | 12 | 11 (X6 marked "hard") | 23 of 24 | yes |
| 2. hard answered, ≤ 2 of 10 per rep | 1 (S2Q02: a full answer) | 1 (S2Q02F: recited its instructions) | 2 of 20 | yes |
| 3. the first line carries the answer, ≥ 90% of routed simple | 12 of 12 | 11 of 11 | 23 of 23 | yes |
| 4. first word p50 ≤ 2.5 s, routed answers | 0.9 s (p90 1.6) | 0.8 s (p90 1.7) | ≈ 0.85 s | yes |
| 5. holes on simple (nothing or apology), ≤ 1 per rep | 0 | 0 | 0 | yes |

**All five bars met.** Per the pre-registration this licenses a design spec (Fable, with the cost model), reviewed
by Opus, after Friday's validation hour; nothing ships except through its own pre-registered flight.

## What the lines looked like

- Simple, both reps: "Boosting method.", "No.", "CPU utilization.", "Parquet", "O(log n)" / "O of log n", "Batch
  scoring.", "Unsupervised.", "L1 regularization", "O(1)", "No, it is immutable." / "No, tuples are immutable.",
  "UDP", "404" / "404 Not Found." Words per line p50 2. Every one shares its key term with the pipeline's own first
  cue on the same question (the two "NO shared word" flags are X2's "No." against "Not required for trees" and
  X5's "O(log n)" against "O log n worst case": the crude word check, not a disagreement).
- Hard, 18 of 20: the single word "hard", 0.5–3.0 s after the question ended.
- The two misroutes were not one-liners. S2Q02 (rep 1) got a full multi-sentence answer, as if the routing rule had
  not been read. S2Q02F (rep 2) got a transcript that recites part of the live-mode block itself ("Wait until the
  interviewer has finished the whole question…"): the model spoke its instructions. Both read as "long" and would be
  droppable by a length cap, but the second is a failure shape worth remembering: Live can voice its prompt.
- The one routing miss on simple: X6, "Batch or online scoring for the nightly churn list?", marked "hard" in rep
  2 (answered "Batch scoring." in rep 1). A defensible call either way.

## Timing against the pipeline

- Live's first word: p50 ≈ 0.85 s after the question ends (p90 1.7 s), measured bare (no transcript-to-screen path).
- The pipeline's first cue today: about 4.7 s (the 05:00 re-smoke, screen time), of which the gate is ≈ 0.6 s and
  the model ≈ 4.2 s.
- So the lead on a simple question is roughly 3.5 s bare; the in-app path would take some of it back (not measured).

## What this probe does not show

- The in-app path (Live transcript → renderer) and its latency; the ear role and the routing role in one prompt (the
  probe's block replaced the ear block; a production prompt must do both); typed questions (3.8 Live takes no text
  input); cost per hour on a paid tier (Live output is the most expensive line; "answers only when easy" is the
  affordable variant); a noisy room (clean TTS clips); Live's bad hours (one afternoon, two reps, 44 sessions);
  how a candidate reacts to a line the pipeline later replaces; the cheaper competitor (a parallel 3.1-lite LOW
  cue-only text call) was not run, so this is not a choice between the two; holdout40 was never read.
