fix(stt): a word lost between two Deepgram finals is restored from the interim

Flight h40c R22: Deepgram's interim held "How do you cut hallucinations
in a rag answer without just making", its finals were "How do you cut"
and "in a rag answer without just making it refuse?", and the app
answered the joined finals without "hallucinations". Streaming cached
clips to Deepgram with the app's live options reproduced it: a final
stops short of its interim and the next final resumes one or two words
later (6 of 20 and 9 of 48 plays lost a word). The loss is Deepgram's;
the interim is the only place the word survives.

deepgramBoundaryRepair remembers such a cut and, when the next final
arrives within 5 s and starts at the interim's second or third word
after it, puts the skipped word(s) back in the interim's spelling. A cut
is the interim's prefix, or the prefix with a re-spelled last word
("RAC" -> "Rag": same first letter, no digit, no longer, so a word
covering more than the interim's, "ninety two percent" -> "92%" or
"all right" -> "Alright", is no re-spelling). No cut is kept when the
interim holds a non-ASCII letter or when the cut final carries
speech_final; an empty final or an UtteranceEnd clears it. A lone word
after the cut is never restored: text alone cannot tell a loss from a
re-hearing there.

DeepgramStreamingSTT creates one repair per socket, only for English
(isEnglishLanguage, now shared with keytermsFor), passes speech_final,
clears on pauses, and logs each repair on the line right after its
final; the golden turns extractor replays that pair as the app saw it
and refuses a repair line that is not right under its own final.

Evidence: the module equals the reference rule event for event on every
recorded run log and the two earlier seam recordings (13,892 events)
and on 45 fixtures. On the non-holdout logs it makes 25 repairs, all
true against the scripted question (25 is a floor on the losses), and 6
in those two recordings. A fresh seam recording of 10 other non-holdout
clips (30 plays, the reference rule under the adapter's wiring) made 1
repair, true, and none wrong. Holdout is no independent validation:
v2's holdout failures chose what v3 removed. Beyond that, no holdout
count was used for any choice (v4's tallies are non-holdout only), and
R22 plus the 4 holdout repairs are excluded from future holdout
measurement of this rule.

Recall is partial: 6 of the 15 seam plays that lost a word were
restored (2 of 9 on the second recording, made after v3 was settled),
and 4 of those 9 left no interim evidence at all. A loss right after an
inflected cut ("scale" -> "scales") also stays unrepaired, a restored
word loses punctuation inside it ("4.1" -> "4 1"), and a strict cut
past a smart_format rewrite (interim "92 percent for each", final
"92%.") would still restore "percent"; neither of the last two occurs
in the data.
