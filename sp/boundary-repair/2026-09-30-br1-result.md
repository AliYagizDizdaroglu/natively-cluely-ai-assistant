## S1Q08 was answered 61 s late

**Symptom.** The question was heard in full and answered a minute after it ended.

**What the log shows** (times in UTC as logged; local is +3 h):
- 11:01:29.972 to 11:01:45.049: five Deepgram finals carry the whole question. It is an instruction ("Design the
  prediction logging, …") with no question mark.
- 11:01:31, :36, :38 and :43: four detector calls on the partial text. None emitted a chip.
- 11:01:45.586 `turn: classify finals=5`; 11:01:45.978 `turn: close reason=not-a-question`. The classify call took
  391 ms.
- 11:01:46.563 the detector's own call on the full text is issued. 11:01:47.373 `chip emitted: intent=coding
  confidence=0.95`, then `dispatch: mark source=whisper verdict=match`. The turn had closed 1.4 s earlier, so the mark
  answered nothing.
- 11:01:55.383 `turn: close reason=nothing-heard`.
- 11:02:46.018 the Live ear's text arrives: `dispatch: mark source=live verdict=unverifiable`, then
  `turn: gate=8397 finals=0 live=1 finished=true`, then `dispatch: answer source=live`. The answer started 4.1 s later.

**Reading.** The same detection model gave two verdicts on the same text 1.4 s apart. The turn machine's classify
call said "not a question" and closed the turn. The detector's call said "question, 0.95" just after. A mark that
arrives after the close is only logged. The Live ear rescued the question a minute later.

**Not caused by the repair, as far as the log shows.** No repair touched this item. At 05:00 the same day, on the
worktree build without the repair, the same question was marked on its first final and answered at the end of the
turn, with no classify call. And `not-a-question` closes are in earlier hours too:
