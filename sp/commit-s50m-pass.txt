docs(passes): s50m — the quality case replicates and the latency condition sinks it

The replication flight for gemini-3.5-flash-lite at HIGH, flown on the rule
pre-registered in passes/PREREGISTER-s50m.md and amended, before takeoff, to
scope the safety condition to the candidate.

On the 18 shared mains the quality case replicated cleanly. 3.1 LOW scored
14/13/15 against 3.5 HIGH's 16/15/17, a mean gain of 2.00 where the rule asked
for 1.0, per-question movement of five to one where it asked for two to one,
and a worst rep of 15 against 13. Conditions 1, 2 and 3 all pass, and the
s50l mains hypothesis is now replicated rather than merely sliced.

Condition 4 fails. Four answers in the live hour crossed the ten-second stall
budget, at 12.8, 15.7, 16.3 and 17.3 seconds, and one more did so in a 3.5
captured arm. No answer was graded wrong anywhere in the live hour or the three
3.5 arms, and the stall race armed correctly on all forty answers with the
right pair and budget, so this is latency rather than a broken fallback.

The rule requires all four. gemini-3.1-flash-lite at LOW therefore stays as the
shipped default, permanently, and the model question closes. No further flight
is spent on it; the remaining flights go to the length lever via the prompt.

Recorded alongside, outside the rule: the length row passes for the first time
at p90 107 words with none over 150, against s50k's p90 159 and five over. The
in-app hour scored 18 of 20 mains, its third consecutive quality-gate pass, but
only 12 of 20 follow-ups, the weakest of the series and below its own captured
band, which is the open question this flight leaves behind.
