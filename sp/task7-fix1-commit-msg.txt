fix(flight): hasCueRule fails closed — every replayable id, not any

.some() over every captured entry let a single cue-carrying prompt gate the
captured-no-cues arms open on a mixed hour, so answers.mjs --no-cues would
then refuse the first id without the rule, mid-flight, instead of the flight
skipping cleanly. hasCueRule now scopes through capturedOnly, exactly what
the arm replays, and requires ALL of those ids to carry the rule, not merely
one; the skip log line now names how many carry it, or lack it, in numbers,
computed once before the loop rather than per arm.
