docs(passes): s50i — the shipped LOW default in flight, in-app 14/20, and the hour's own five paired arms

First flight on the shipped default (commit d2a6c72: thinking level LOW with no environment
override). The hour was mechanically clean: 40/40 heard and answered, 0 doubles, 6 supersedes,
15/15 long questions whole, 40/40 prompts pinned, 0 coaching-path answers. Every one of the 50
verbal requests logged thinking=LOW and 48 of them actually spent thought tokens (p50 791,
max 1181), so the shipped default is proven in flight. TTFT p90 9.1 s passes the 10 s gate bar
this change also moved, and would have failed the old 5 s bar.

Grades, mains of 20 / follow-ups of 20: in-app 14/15. Zero wrong answers on either axis, the
same as s50g at LOW; every miss is a weak, and 28 of the 40 answers run past ~85 words.

The five paired arms (b18b8fa, bd2bea3, bfefef0) ran minutes after the hour, so every read
below is same-window and on shared question ids:

  level, app bytes, offline       captured-minimal 28/39 -> captured-low 33/39   net +5, 7 up 2 down
  level, bare bytes               bare 3.1 16/20 -> bare 3.1 LOW 19/20           net +3, 4 up 1 down
  app context at LOW              bare LOW 19/20 -> in-app 14/20                 net -5, 0 up 5 down
  app pipeline over a raw call    captured-low 33/39 -> in-app 28/39             net -5, 2 up 7 down
  3.1 LOW vs 3.5 HIGH, app bytes  33/39 vs 34/39                                 net +1, 4 up 3 down
  3.1 LOW vs 3.5 HIGH, bare bytes 19/20 vs 18/20                                 net -1, 1 up 2 down

The level result reproduces the 2026-09-17 bench in a live hour. The two 3.5 reads reproduce the
2026-09-18 bench: a tie, now measured in the same window rather than across times of day, which
was that bench's one unresolved confound. Same-window latency on app bytes: 3.1 LOW p50 4.6 s /
p90 7.0 s / max 12.6 s against 3.5 HIGH 4.2 / 5.0 / 6.7 s; words p50 99 against 74.

The two negative reads are the finding worth acting on. The app's own context and pipeline each
cost about as much as the thinking level buys, and they cost it on the same handful of questions
(S1Q02, S1Q06, S1Q08, S2Q05, S2Q07). That is now measured rather than inferred.
