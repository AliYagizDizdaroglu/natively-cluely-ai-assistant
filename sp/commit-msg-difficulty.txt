feat(detector): ask the detection model for a difficulty and log it

The Groq detection schema (strict) gains difficulty: easy | medium |
hard, defined in one prompt line (definition/recall; mechanism or
trade-off; system design, several constraints, or multi-part). The
validated response carries it when present; a value outside the enum is
dropped with a warning; clients that do not ask for it (Ollama, Gemini)
leave it undefined. QuestionDetector prints it in the "detect returned"
line and copies it onto the chip. Nothing routes on it: after9 collects
the per-question evidence first (spec 2026-09-07 §3, routing question).
