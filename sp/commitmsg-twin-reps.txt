feat(flight): run the offline twin three times, so the hour carries its own noise floor

Flight s50i put the live hour at 28/39 against its offline twin's 33/39 — identical bytes,
identical model, identical thinking level. Only one of the seven questions that moved had an
identifiable mechanical cause (the word guard, fixed in 01d3810); the other six differ only by
sampling at temperature 0.4. At one sample a side, against a bench noise floor of ±4 on 38
pairs, that gap cannot be told apart from chance.

So captured-low now runs three times (captured-low, -r2, -r3), identical but for the tag. The
three reps measure the twin's own rep-to-rep spread, and that band is what the single live hour
has to fall outside before the gap counts as a pipeline defect rather than sampling. Adds about
78 lite calls; both models stay well inside their daily quotas.

This is the cheapest way to settle the largest open number in the flight, and it settles it
without spending three flight hours.
