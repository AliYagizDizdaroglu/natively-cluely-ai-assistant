## What the lines show

- **The router costs the ear 15 question captures in a continuous session** (54 → 39 on the same clips and shape). The
  combined prompt lost the tool call on nearly every follow-up (22 of 23 missing in V1, against 7 in the control)
  and on two easy questions it answered without calling the tool (E04, E20). In the app a missed tool call falls
  back to the Deepgram detector (slower, the known doubles), so this is a regression on the ear's main job.
- **The garbled lines come from tool-call loops.** Each of V1's three garbled lines follows the model calling
  `handle_question` 3–5 times in a row, then speaking a stray word. Speech and the tool in one turn misbehave on
  3.8 Live. A 5-word cap does not catch "maintaining"; only a whitelist ("hard" or a line sharing a term with the
  question) would.
- **Routing quality depends on session shape, the opposite way from what I expected.** The continuous session got
  20/20 easy right; fresh per-chain sessions said "hard" to 6 easy questions. One rep each.
- **The answer-based follow-up hole reappears:** V2's H05F "What's the time complexity of your rate limiter?" →
  "O(1)." is L38F's AF1 word for word; H18F "Can you do it in a single pass?" → "Yes." (right by luck: the
  pipeline's own answer was already single-pass).
- **The ear-only 3.8 Live speaks junk** despite "never speak" (27 spoken lines, mostly "<no speech>{pause}", "---").
  The app ignores the ear's speech, so this matters only to the separate 3.8 Live ear trial; that trial should also
  note 54/63 capture with 7 follow-ups missed (the app's own ear today is 3.1 Live, not measured here).

## What it means for the router design

- The production shape (ear + router in one prompt) fails on the ear, which the whole app depends on. A router
  would need its OWN Live session beside the ear (a second Live session on a paid tier doubles the biggest cost
  line) or a text call fed by the ear's question.
- Where it works, it works well: 24/25 easy items right at 1.3 s in one continuous session, 0 reconnects.
- The text-call competitor (question text from the ear + the on-screen answer → a cue-only 3.1-lite LOW line) has
  none of the three failure shapes (no tool loop, no ear cost, sees the answer) and is the next thing to measure.
