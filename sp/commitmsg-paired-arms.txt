feat(flight): two paired arms per hour — the captured prompts at MINIMAL, the bare prompt at LOW

The 2026-09-17 bench found the thinking-level effect (+21 acceptable on 117 pairs) and
the app-context tax (+6 on 57) only in paired grading on the same bytes; arms compared
across hours sit inside the +/-4 noise. Every flight now produces its own pairs on the
app's answer model:

  captured-minimal  the hour's captured prompts (system + user turn, follow-ups included)
                    replayed with no thinkingConfig, the provider default the app sent
                    through s50f. In-app vs this = the level, same bytes.
  low               the bare verbal prompt at LOW, the shipped level. In-app vs this = the
                    app context at the shipped level; low vs the plain bare arm = the level
                    on bare bytes.

answers.mjs gains --thinking <MINIMAL|LOW|MEDIUM|HIGH> (needs --tag; Gemini only; a bad
level is refused before any call) and keeps each answer's reported thoughtsTokenCount so an
arm proves the level it actually got. flight.mjs lists the arms as PAIRED_ARMS, files them as
<model>_<tag> the way answers.mjs and the judge already name tagged arms, moves their old
files aside, skips the captured arm loudly when the hour left no capture, and adds their
pairs files to the done marker's grading list. ~60 lite calls per flight.
