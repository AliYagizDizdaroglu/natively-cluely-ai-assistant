fix(live): ask the Live listener for the whole question, not "one clear sentence"

Measured on the six long design questions of the 2026-09-08 roster, each
clip written as PCM into the real GeminiLiveRouter (scratchpad
record-long.mjs, three runs on the same clips):

  prompt                                   whole (cov >= 0.8)  events  after clip
  "as one clear sentence" (before)          0 of 6              1 each  1.0-1.5 s
  + include the scenario sentences          5 of 6              L02 x2  0.15-0.8 s
  + do not fire while a scenario is still   6 of 6              1 each  0.24-1.35 s
    being set up; report it once (this)

Before, Live returned 23-35 of 69-84 words (the last sentence), so the
answer never saw the constraints. The control (calibrate-audio: W02, M02,
H03, the C01 cue) is unchanged: each fired once, heard 92-100%. Mid-clip
fires on long questions remain stochastic (2 of 18 long plays across the
new wordings; the whole question still follows), which is why the
calibration now scores a long item by its best hearing and passes it on
at least one fire.

names the Groq-key requirement for the two Groq arms.
