revert(turn): the wordless-final signal is not one Deepgram sends reliably

This reverts a565914. Its unit proof held; its live proof did not. The same
noisy smoke (a 1 s tone twice in every 14 s gap) ran before and after:

  before (old build)   1 answer, 2 supersedes of 4   Deepgram finalized each
                       tone EMPTY (is_final=true, text="") ~1.4 s after it
  after  (a565914)     1 answer, 3 supersedes of 4   Deepgram sent an
                       utterance-end for tone 1, NOTHING for tone 2, and the
                       only empty final of the gap arrived mid-question with
                       the voice on — where the method is a no-op by design

An empty final is something Deepgram emits sometimes for a wordless segment,
not every time. A rule that waits for it closes the turn on the runs where it
comes and swallows the next question on the runs where it does not. The
reliable evidence is the ABSENCE of a worded final after a voice stop, which
needs nothing new from Deepgram; the next commit clocks the turn on that.
