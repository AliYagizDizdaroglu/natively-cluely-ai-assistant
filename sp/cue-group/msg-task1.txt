fix(cues): close the cue block on the first prose character, not the first prose line

stripCueBlock read complete lines only inside the block, so a one-paragraph
spoken answer held its cues and the whole paragraph until the stream ended:
15 of 21 answers at the 16:12 re-smoke and 14 of 22 at 05:00 appeared in one
piece (final review I2). The block now closes as soon as the pending partial
line can no longer become a cue line — anything but digits, digits and
whitespace, or digits and a bar (CUE_LINE_PREFIX, with CUE_LINE's own
whitespace after the bar so a CR there is held, tested on the trimmed partial
so a CRLF boundary cannot release a cue line as prose). A partial cue line is
still held to its newline. The parser's cues and its joined prose are the same
for every input; only the report's moment and the pieces move. Pinned: the
review's case, a chunk-size matrix on a paragraph with no newline, every
partial state, and the two seam states a shown or held first chunk creates
(a failure after the words were shown is an error, not a redirect — MAIN's
contract; one before they clear the filters is a redirect under the dead
stream's cue).
