fix(cues): an offers block before the spoken answer no longer swallows the answer

The model sometimes writes its __MORE__ offers right after the cue block and
before the spoken answer (9 of 366 saved 3.5-lite replies lead with the
block, 8 of them with an answer after it, and 1 of 247 on 3.1-lite; the
16:12 re-smoke's block-only answer). stripSuggestionBlock treated everything after
the sentinel as the block, so the answer was thrown away and the candidate
read "Could you repeat that?" with no cues. When the sentinel arrives before
any spoken text the block is now only the run of offer lines and blank lines
after it; the answer is released at its first character that cannot start an
offer line (CUE_LINE_PREFIX, the early close's expression) and the normal
path resumes, so a later sentinel still ends it. extractSuggestions gets the
same leading branch. Answer-first streams are byte-identical, offers-only
stays empty, the callback fires once, and one console.warn line per stream
names a leading block so a run can show the branch fired. Pinned on 40 cases:
every partial state after the offers, the release chunk at four sizes, the
whole-vs-streaming matrix, the chain from the cue block to the word budget,
and the redirect window a leading block opens.
