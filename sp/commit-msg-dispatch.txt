feat(dispatch): answered questions stay deduped for 60 s; the fuller sentence of an answered head is answered

Two pipeline findings of the 2026-09-06 weak-answer spike, replayed offline
over the after5/after6/after7 flight logs before being wired in:

1. Re-fires. Live 3.1 re-fires a question it already had answered 20-26 s
   later (after7: M04 at 20.0 s, M27 at 26 s), past ChipDeduper's 20 s window,
   so the hour got two answers. An ANSWERED entry now stays authoritative for
   60 s (answeredWindowMs); unanswered chips still expire at 20 s. Past 20 s a
   text with fewer than four content words on either side never matches by
   containment/Jaccard — after5's answered "I'm going to go." would otherwise
   have swallowed the next real question at +48 s (the replay's one false
   suppression). Replay: exactly the two after7 doubles removed, zero false
   suppressions.

2. Extend on an added clause. The STT closes a final at a mid-question pause
   ("What is a DAG?" … "and why does Airflow use that structure?"): the head
   is answered at once and the fuller sentence is dropped as a duplicate, its
   second half never answered (after7 W10, M27). shouldExtend
   (services/extendOnClause.ts) fires when the later text CONTAINS the
   answered text and adds at least three words within 30 s; dispatchDetection
   then answers the fuller question (hands-free only), logs
   "dispatch: extend … extends=… question=…", and ChipDeduper.extend() makes
   the entry stand for the fuller sentence so a later re-fire of it is a plain
   duplicate. Replay over 180 answers: 21 firings, 5 on heads the judge had
   marked weak or wrong, 16 on heads judged acceptable whose second clause the
   interviewer did ask; content-word variants miss M27's "and when would you
   not?".

Harness: the metrics parse the extend action (one surface, not a double;
extendsTotal on the surfaced row) and the judge export appends an extension's
answer to its head pair, so the judge grades what the candidate would say.
Re-running the after7 gate is unchanged: 2 doubles, 0 extended.
