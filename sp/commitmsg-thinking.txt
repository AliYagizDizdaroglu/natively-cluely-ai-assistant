feat(verbal): thinking level for the Gemini answer from NATIVELY_GEMINI_THINKING_LEVEL

Every flight through s50f sent no thinkingConfig on the verbal request, which on
gemini-3.1-flash-lite is MINIMAL: probes on the app's own captured S1Q02 call
(2026-09-15) reported no thought tokens and the CV-numbers derivation wrong,
matching the flights (0 of 10 across five hours). thinkingLevel LOW on the same
bytes spent 143 and 782 thought tokens and derived the 57 percent precision and
30 percent recall both times. Flights s50g (LOW) and s50h (MEDIUM) fly the same
hour with the level as the only variable.

The level comes from the environment like the turn constants: unset sends
nothing (what every earlier flight sent), one of MINIMAL/LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH is put
on the request for non-Gemma Gemini models (primary and fallback alike), and
anything else is refused at startup with the allowed values named, so an hour
never runs at a level nobody asked for. The last stream chunk's usage is logged
per answer ("usage: thinking=LOW thoughts=143 out=160 in=5157"), which is the
per-answer proof of what the model actually spent; thoughtsTokenCount is absent
when it did not think, so the line shows 0.

Tests: env helper (unset, four levels case-insensitively, refusal) and the
verbal call (no thinkingConfig when unset; LOW on the request with temperature
and cap unchanged; the usage line). Watched fail first. tsc at the 6-error
baseline; 673 tests green.
