fix(golden): let a failed app start say why, and give it room to start

The 19:50 model-override smoke died with an exit code and two absent logs and
nothing else. appStart spawned the app with stdio 'ignore', so the one message
that could have named the cause — the single-instance-lock line at main.ts:3391,
a native-module load failure, a modal dialog — went nowhere. Both streams now
land in interview60.runs/app-start.log, and both failure paths name that file.

The budget goes from 90s to 180s in the same breath. The s50k start that worked
this morning reached whenReady 55s into the old 90s; the smoke tonight was still
short of it when the budget expired, having got past Vite and a clean
build:electron. Three times the known-good time turns a slow start into a pass
and still leaves a genuine hang plainly over budget.

Calibrated rather than assumed: a detached child with this exact stdio shape
writes stdout AND stderr to the file, with our copy of the fd closed straight
after the spawn.
