on the auto turn path only, adds the immediately preceding question when a follow-up cannot stand alone and its parent
is gone from the prompt (one line, "asked earlier" label, any failure = today's bytes). Written by Fable, three Opus
review rounds applied; the user's five decisions are in section 11 ("all recommended"). Nothing is built before its
pre-registered replay (188 calls) passes.
