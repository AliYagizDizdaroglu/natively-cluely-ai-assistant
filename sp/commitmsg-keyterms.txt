feat(stt): keyterm-prompt Deepgram with the roster terms it measurably loses

Flight s50j's only wrong answer was not the model's. Deepgram heard "while preserving the tie
rule" as "the Thai rule", and the model answered the garble — in every offline arm too, since
they all replay the same transcript. An STT defect scored as a quality miss.

The list is measured, not guessed. Diffing each scripted roster question against the text the
app actually heard for it (233 scripted-vs-heard pairs across every run we still hold) and
keeping only words lost in two or more separate flights leaves a short list; hyphenation and
numeral rewrites ("dead-letter", "million") were excluded as smart_format artefacts the model
reads correctly anyway. Two of the survivors change an answer's meaning rather than its
spelling — the tie rule above, and S2Q05's "deduplicate chunk ids" heard as "duplicate chunk
IDs", which inverts the requirement.

Verified against the original symptom, by replaying the clips through a real nova-3 live
socket at the app's own settings (16 kHz linear16, smart_format, endpointing 300), list off
then on:

    FIXED      "Thai rule"   -> "tie rule"    S1Q04F, the answer-changing one
    FIXED      "re ranking"  -> "reranking"   S2Q01
    NOT FIXED  "axe" for AKS                  S1Q09F
    NOT FIXED  "documenteds" for document ids S2Q04

The two that do not respond were chased rather than assumed: six keyterm spellings each, and
the decoder returns "axe" at 0.64-0.74 confidence every time. The synthetic roster audio
genuinely says "axe", so no keyterm can reach it — a human interviewer saying the letters
would not produce this. Both terms stay in the list for that case, documented as unverified
rather than counted as fixes.

keyterm is a nova-3 English-only parameter and the app switches language at runtime, so the
list rides only on English sockets; every other language, 'multi' included, sends nothing.
