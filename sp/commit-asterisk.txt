cleanNotation removed "**" and stopped there, so italic emphasis reached the
ear verbatim. Flight s50m, 2026-09-22: S1Q03F spoke "the *incremental* gain"
and took the hour's only delivery 0 for it.

The rule is judged the way the "$" rule is judged, by the character on the
inside. A star touching a non-space is a delimiter and goes; a star with space
on both sides is multiplication or a stray bullet and stays. The trailing-"*"
hold in stripSpokenNotation already existed for exactly this decision, and its
comment already promised it: only the rule was missing.

Two things the first attempt got wrong, both caught before this commit.

A closing star released at the head of the next span has nothing behind it for
the lookbehind to see, so it survived. That is the same problem the "$" rule
solved with lastOut, and the helper now covers both delimiters. Extending that
helper then broke a stream ENDING in bold: it sliced one star off a held "**"
and left an orphan no rule could match, which the full suite caught and a
single-file run would not have. The helper now claims a lone star only.

Calibrated against every answer the harness has recorded rather than against
the test alone: 4460 answers across 19 runs, 33 containing a star, 31 of them
fixed by this rule, and zero stars anywhere in that corpus sit between two
digits. The leak was never a one-off — it has been in every flight on record
and simply cost a grade for the first time.
