fix(verbal): race the first token on the technical route too, with a thinking-aware budget

Flight s50h (2026-09-16) waited 44 s and 60 s for a first token on two technical answers
and the 4 s fallback never fired: zero "stalled after" lines in the hour, and the 60 s
answer (S2Q09) landed after the harness stopped attributing it. The race lived only in
streamVerbalWithGeminiFlash, the behavioral route, which answers one question an hour;
the 40 technical answers go through streamChat's verbal branch, which streamed the
primary with no clock.

The race is now one private generator, streamGeminiWithStallFallback, called by both
routes: same fallback choice (the other Flash Lite), same source sentinel, errors from
the primary still propagate — only silence is caught. The stall warning names the
primary that stalled instead of the Flash Lite constant.

The budget comes from firstTokenTimeoutMs: 4000 ms with no thinking level or MINIMAL
(first token p90 1.6 s on s50e, 3.4 s on s50f), 10000 ms once a level above MINIMAL is
set — s50g at LOW had p50 5.1 s and p90 7.8 s, so a 4 s race would have replaced most of
that hour's thinking answers with the fallback's default-level ones.
NATIVELY_FIRST_TOKEN_TIMEOUT_MS overrides both (the live smoke forces the race with
300 ms); anything but a positive whole number is refused.

Tests: the budget function (default, MINIMAL, LOW/HIGH, override, refusal) and the
technical route through streamChat under fake timers — a silent primary falls back at
4 s with the sentinel and the same systemInstruction on the fallback call, an answering
primary is not touched, LOW extends the budget to 10 s, the override to 300 ms logs the
stall line, and the behavioral route keeps its race. Watched fail first (three cases
hung past 5 s, the function was missing). tsc at the 6-error baseline.
