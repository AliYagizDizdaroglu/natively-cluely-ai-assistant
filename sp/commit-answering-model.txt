fix(verbal): name the model that answered, and fall back from the one that failed

Flights s50l and s50m answered on gemini-3.5-flash-lite through the verbal
override, and three things about that model's name were wrong, all silently:

- The bar under every answer said gemini-3.1-flash-lite. generateStream
  named the selected model while LLMHelper answered on the override.
- The stall race's switch never reached the bar. LLMHelper announces it as
  the head sentinel of the stream it hands back, and stripModelSentinel
  strips head sentinels: s50m's verbal-diag.log shows all four of the
  hour's switches stripped within 2 ms of the stall.
- A 503 before the first token was "redirected" to 3.5-lite again. The
  error fallback named a constant, and streamVerbalWithGeminiFlash
  re-resolved it through the override, which ignores what it is given.
  A second 503 meant no answer at all.

The label is now resolved the way the call is, the switch is read off the
raw stream and named again outside the chain, and the error fallback picks
the Flash Lite that did not just fail, at the moment it fails. The override
picks the primary only: a caller that names its model gets that model,
which also keeps typed chat on its selected model, as the override's own
header already promised.

Live, on the built modules with the real SDK (one 503 injected at fetch,
one stall forced with a 50 ms budget): before, 3.5-lite answered under a
3.1-lite label, a 503 retried 3.5-lite, and the stall switch was eaten.
After, each names and reaches the right model, and the shipped pairing
(3.1-lite failing over to 3.5-lite) is unchanged.
