fix(knowledge): a question with a technical marker is never a negotiation

STRONG_NEGOTIATION carried the bare word "salary", so "the SQL for the second highest salary in
each department" (h40a R09, 2026-09-24) was answered with the negotiation coaching card. Two
changes: the bare word becomes the phrases an interviewer uses about the candidate's own pay,
and a question that carries a technical marker (sql and the transcript's spellings sequel,
postgresql, postgres, mysql; query/queries, tables, database/databases, schema/schemas,
column/columns, function/functions, algorithm/algorithms, regression, pipeline/pipelines,
dataset/datasets, implement, code) is never a negotiation whatever pay term it contains. "table"
and "join" are not markers: "on the table" and "join us" are negotiation idioms.

Trade-off, recorded: a negotiation question worded with "salary" but none of the phrases ("What's
your current salary?", "How flexible are you on salary?"), or one that carries a marker as an
ordinary word ("Any queries about the compensation package?", "There are other candidates in the
pipeline", "the finance function", "your salary expectations for this senior Postgres role"), now
gets an ordinary answer instead of the coaching card, and
without the salary block the coaching path added. A wrong card on a technical question is
unspeakable; a plain answer to a pay question is not. Against the previous HEAD the change only
removes negotiation classifications (0 exceptions over the 257 roster questions).
