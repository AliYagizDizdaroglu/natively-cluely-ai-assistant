fix(turn): a voice stop that no words follow is not the interviewer resuming

Symptom (smoke 2026-09-13 21:50 UTC; reproduced on demand 2026-09-14 with a
1 s tone twice in every 14 s gap): the first question answered, the next ones
emitted as `dispatch: supersede` carrying the previous question joined with
the new — 1 answer and 2-3 supersedes for 4 questions; `finals=` on the turn
lines grew across questions because the first turn never closed.

Root cause. After a dispatch the continuation close waited for 8 s with no
VAD off-transition. Energy on the desktop loopback that is not speech (a
chime, another participant, the tone) restarts that clock from every blip's
stop; the next question begins before it expires and its finals are appended
to the answered turn as a continuation. Ruled out: Deepgram's speech-started
(logs only), the microphone (all three VAD feeds are systemAudioCapture),
empty finals as pending text (dropped upstream). Not a gap-length effect.
Replay could not see it: fixture voice windows come from the clip WAVs, whose
floor is digital silence.

Fix, in the machine only. A voice stop counts toward the continuation clocks
only while its words could still be on the way; once wordlessGraceMs (3 s)
has passed with no worded final, it was not speech and drops out of the
close rule and R15's staleness rule. Provenance for 3 s: on the 116 golden
questions the last final follows the last voice stop by p50 708 ms, p99
1029 ms, max 1041 ms; 3 s is ~3x the max. A worded final after the dispatch
flips pendingAfterDispatch, so every stop these clocks see is one nobody's
words followed yet. The close is therefore the later of dispatch + 8 s and
last stop + 3 s; nextTimerAt names both. Named residual: a question whose
first worded final lands inside 3 s of a blip's stop still joins the answered
turn (the question would have to start almost as the blip ends).

Why not the empty-final signal a565914 used: reverted in e15ef7d — the live
after-run showed Deepgram sends it for some wordless blips and not others.

Tests: three cases (blips -> close at the grace -> the next question's own
dispatch; a question starting right after a blip is its own turn even with
its first final under 8 s after the blip's stop; a short real continuation
whose words land 0.8 s after its stop still supersedes) plus the constants
table. Watched fail first; with the old rule restored the first two fail on
behaviour. Replay unchanged: 116/116 once, 0 supersedes. tsc at the 6-error
baseline.
