fix(router): pad the router's input with silence up to real time (only in silence)

The smoke (2026-10-07 01:03) decided 43 of 47 turns "late": the 3.8 router's first word came 3.0-3.8 s after the
question ended, against 0.6-1.3 s in the standalone probe. The native SilenceSuppressor sends one zero-filled 20 ms
keepalive frame per 100 ms after its hangover, and the router was fed that thinned stream, so Gemini Live's
server-side end of turn (counted in audio time) arrived seconds late in wall time. LiveRouterSession now pads zero
PCM up to wall-clock time, but only around all-zero chunks, so a main-thread stall during speech never injects
silence mid-question. The ear is unchanged.

Integration commits bf26b08 + 7408aac; Opus-reviewed (APPROVE after one round). LiveRouterSession 27/27,
routerWiring 24/24; tsc root 0, electron 6 (pre-existing).
