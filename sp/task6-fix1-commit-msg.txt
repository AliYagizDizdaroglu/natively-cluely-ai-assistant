test(golden): a drift test for the cue row's hardcoded limits

interview60.metrics.mjs deliberately imports no build, so wellformedCues
hardcodes CUE_MAX_LINES/CUE_MAX_WORDS as the literals 5 and 8 — nothing
would notice if they drifted from electron/llm/prompts.ts, and the
cueBlocks gate row would silently keep gating on stale numbers. The new
test builds its fixture from the real constants (a block at exactly the
line/word limits, one line over, one word over) so it exercises whatever
the limits actually are, not today's numbers restated. Calibrated: with
the literal 5 mutated to 4 the new test fails (wellformed 1 -> 0); restored,
it passes.
