fix(verbal): stream whole answers under a 200-word guard instead of the question-scaled cut

Flight s50c (2026-09-12) scored 12/20 in-app against 18/20 for the same
model, same prompt, called bare. The remaining gap is the app's own cut:
spokenWordBudget clamped the stream at clamp(80, 2.5 x question words, 150),
fired on 13 of 42 in-app answers, and removed the LAST asked part from 4 of
the 8 in-app failures (S2Q01 stops after reranking, S2Q07 loses evaluation,
S2Q08 loses cutover/rollback, S2Q02 loses the dataset). Applied offline to
the bare arm's own 20 answers the cut truncates 7 (224 words), including
exactly the S2Q08 paragraph the grader marked missing; across all four
same-hour arms it would cut 15 of 80, and the arms' longest answer is 198
words.

The prompt keeps asking for 80-150 words; the model's own stop is what
earns 18/20. The stream is now only clamped at the 200 the gate row already
bounds: SPOKEN_WORD_GUARD = { limit: 200, floor: 200, ceiling: 200 }, a hard
stop that never fires on a real answer (0 of 80 offline). The gate row
"Spoken answers: streamed whole under the 200-word guard" now fails on ANY
cut, so a runaway is reported instead of hidden.

Also: the notation filter strips a LaTeX percentage ("$9.5\%$" -> "9.5%",
3.5 Flash on s50c) while keeping "$9.5 per user" as money; the held tail
now covers a backslash after "$<number>" so the result is chunk-invariant.

Tests: guard clamp/whole-stream cases, wiring (170 words whole for a 5- or
60-word question, 230 clamped at 200, coding exempt), gate row (any cut
fails, p50 unbounded), notation percentage + chunk invariance. Full suite
646 passed.
