docs(passes): pre-register the spoken-answer latency test, before any window runs

s50m's condition 4 failed on four stall fallbacks. With a 10 s budget, every
fallback that fires puts its answer past 10 s, so the condition could not
tell the model from the safety net. The same-window probe s50m asked for,
run on 2026-09-22, pointed the other way and generated this hypothesis. It
does not count toward the verdict.

The rule, approved today:
- Three windows on 2026-09-23, at 10:15, 15:00 and 21:00.
- Both models on s50m's 39 captured prompts, back to back and alternating.
- Each prompt is charged what the user would wait, by the app's own
  mechanics.

3.5-lite HIGH passes only if its median wait is within 750 ms of 3.1-lite
LOW's, its p90 wait is within 1000 ms, and it forces the fallback on at most
6 more of the 117 prompts. On a pass, 3.5-lite HIGH becomes the spoken
answer's default. On a fail, 3.1-lite LOW stays and the question closes.

The margins were set from the null alone, and the rule's reach is stated in
advance: equal models pass 97.6 %, reversed data fails 100 %, a 15 % stall
rate fails 94.5 %, and s50m's 10 % is a coin flip.
