docs(stt): record the keyterm regression sweep, and the control that reinterprets it

Keyterm prompting biases the decoder toward the terms it is given, so the list could make
questions it never mentions worse. That risk was documented in the module and not tested.

All 40 s50j clips replayed through the live socket off and on, scored by word error rate
against the scripted text: mean WER 22.35% off, 22.43% on, 35 of 40 clips byte-identical. The
sweep flagged 2 clips worse and 3 better — at ONE run per config, which is precisely the
single-sample reading the s50j twin reps exist to forbid.

Three reps per config on those five settles it. S1Q08's 17.1-point "regression" reproduces in
full with the list OFF: its within-config spread is 17.1 points, because this harness's fixed
6 s tail sometimes truncates the clip (24 words captured instead of 31 — the words it does
capture are identical). S1Q06's "regression" and S1Q07's "improvement" both vanish at 0.0
spread and 0.0 difference. Only S2Q01 moves, -7.7 points with the list on, stable across 3 of
3 reps — the reranking recovery.

So the list costs nothing measurable and its gains are reproducible. The word-error-rate
metric was itself calibrated first against seven known cases (identical, substitution,
deletion, insertion, empty, punctuation-only, unrelated text).
