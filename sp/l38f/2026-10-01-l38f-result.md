## What the lines show

- **Question-based follow-ups: 12 of 12 right, first word p50 1.0 s.** "Order of n." / "O(n) in the worst case.",
  "No, they are immutable.", "500", "It takes linear time.", "No.", "L2 regularization." Live used the parent
  question it heard earlier in the session every time. One rep-1 line came back in SPANISH ("No, las tuplas son
  inmutables."): correct, and a language-drift defect.
- **Answer-based follow-ups: the dangerous shape appeared.** Rep 2 AF1, "What's the time complexity of your
  approach?" after a rate-limiter design it had marked hard: "O(1) constant time.", a confident one-liner about an
  approach the candidate never stated. Rep 2 AF5, "Was that the right call?": "Absolutely, it was the right call. It
  drastically improved our system's scalability…", an invented experience answer. Rep 1 AF4: a full answer about RAG
  metrics. AF2 and AF3 ("How long did it take you?", "Which data store would you use for that?") were "hard" both reps.
- **Hard parents: 12 of 12 "hard".**
- **Roster control (reported):** real scenario50 pairs were answered in full instead of "hard" in 3 of 8 (rep 1:
  S1Q10, S1Q03F, S1Q10F) and 1 of 8 (rep 2: S1Q10F) items, worse than L38R's 2 of 20 on the hard set. The longer
  live-mode block may have loosened routing; not tested apart.
- **Malformed "hard":** `hard"".hard`, `hard"hard`, `<system>\nhard\n</system>`: a production parser would need to
  read these as hard; the markup leak is a second sign Live can voice its instructions (L38R S2Q02F).

## Reliability

32 sessions per rep plus retries: rep 1 none abnormal; rep 2 the 1006 close + failed reconnect at 15:58 (the user's
local disconnect) and one Google 1011 at 16:02:20 (AP4, retried and completed).

## What it means

- Live answers easy follow-ups that lean on the earlier QUESTION quickly and correctly, so its session memory works.
- Told explicitly that it never hears the candidate, it still answered answer-dependent follow-ups 4 times in 12
  (3 counting only genuine answers), including a confident wrong-risk one-liner and an invented experience. A prompt
  rule does not hold that line; the design cannot rely on it.
- By the pre-registration: follow-ups stay on the pipeline in the design spec; the answer-dependent risk is recorded.
  The text-call competitor (given the earlier question AND the on-screen answer) is the way to cover follow-ups.
